Tips to Get the Best AI Product Photos

Shoot in good lighting

Natural daylight gives the cleanest results. Avoid harsh shadows. If shooting indoors, place your product near a large window during the day.

Use multiple angles

Upload front, side, and detail shots separately. This gives you a complete gallery that shows customers everything they need to see before buying.

Test different styles

What works for a candle (warm, cozy lifestyle) is different from what works for a phone case (clean, minimal, tech-focused). Generate a few versions and test which one gets more clicks.
